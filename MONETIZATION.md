# ScanIQ — Monetization Strategy

## Revenue Streams
1. **Subscription Model (Primary)**
   - **Pro Monthly ($4.99)**: Unlimited history, Cloud Sync, AI "Explain" credits.
   - **Pro Yearly ($39.99)**: 33% discount + custom QR branding features.
2. **One-Time Purchases (Secondary)**
   - **Branding Pack ($9.99)**: Lifetime unlock for custom QR colors/logos.
3. **Advertising (Tertiary)**
   - Minimal native ads for Free tier users after every 5 scans.

## Pricing Strategy Validation
- **Freemium Tier**: Limit history to 50 scans to drive "Pro" conversion for power users.
- **Trial Period**: 7-day free trial for the Yearly plan to reduce friction.

## Key Metrics (KPIs)
- **LTV (Lifetime Value)**: Target $15+ per subscriber.
- **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)**: Target < $3 per active user.
- **Conversion Rate**: Target 3% from Free to Pro.
- **Retention**: Focus on "Scan Daily" streak features to reduce churn.

## User Acquisition Plan
- **SEO/ASO**: Focus on keywords like "Safe QR Scanner", "Fast Barcode App", "AI QR Scanner".
- **Viral Loop**: "Share App" feature rewarded with temporary Pro credits.
- **Content Marketing**: Blog posts about QR security and business use-cases.
